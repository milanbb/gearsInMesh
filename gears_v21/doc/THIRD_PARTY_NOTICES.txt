KELLEY NONLINEAR SOLVERS

The bundled classes/private/nsolve.m adapts and combines routines by
C. T. (Tim) Kelley, distributed in John Burkardt's KELLEY collection:
https://people.sc.fsu.edu/~jburkardt/m_src/kelley/kelley.html

Upstream solvers:
  nsol.m  - Newton/Shamanskii/Chord
  nsola.m - Newton-Krylov-Armijo
https://people.sc.fsu.edu/~jburkardt/m_src/kelley/nsol.m
https://people.sc.fsu.edu/~jburkardt/m_src/kelley/nsola.m

Associated upstream routines include diffjac, dirder, fdkrylov, fdgmres,
fdcgstab, fdtfqmr, givapp and parab3p. Each of these source files identifies
Tim Kelley as author and explicitly states the MIT licence (checked
2 October 2026). Retain the applicable author and permission notices when
redistributing the adapted source.
Licence terms: https://opensource.org/license/mit

The local file is named nsolve.m. It combines the two solver paths and adds
name-value options, analytic-Jacobian Newton, root polishing and diagnostics.
It is an adaptation, not an unchanged copy or a simple file rename.
This notice identifies the upstream licence; it does not assign a licence
to unrelated files in the gears package.

Algorithm reference:
C. T. Kelley, Iterative Methods for Linear and Nonlinear Equations,
SIAM, 1995. ISBN 978-0-89871-352-7.
https://doi.org/10.1137/1.9781611970944

External bibliographic references in verification scripts are sources of
comparison data, not bundled publications or additional runtime dependencies.
